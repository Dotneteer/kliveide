# Designing a party invitation

This chapter will explain how to:

- Type in text
- Make corrections to the text you have entered
- Create bold and underlined text
- Alter the layout of text
- Save (and load) the document
- Print the document

## Starting PipeDream

This example shows how PipeDream can be used in a typical word-processing application. To enter PipeDream from the Index starting with a blank document, select PipeDream from the Index, and press the <Key k="enter" /> key. Alternatively use the short-form codes displayed on the right-hand side of the application's name, in this case by typing <Key k="square" />P.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/index-pipedream.webp" alt="index-pipedream.png" width={670} />

## Typing text

Type the following:

<Typed>You are invited to a beano.</Typed>

Notice that the cursor moves to the right as you type. The next character you type will always be typed at the position indicated by the cursor.

A short time after you stop typing, you will see the page map, on the right-hand side of the screen, change to show the new layout of text on your page.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/pipedream-invitation1.webp" alt="pipedream-invitation1.png" width={670} />

## Deleting

Press the <Key k="del" /> key at the top right-hand side of the keyboard, until the word `beano` disappears. <Key k="del" /> backspaces the cursor over the text you have typed, removing it from the page.

## Word wrap

Type the word party and then continue to type the paragraph shown below. If you make a mistake, use the <Key k="del" /> key to correct it.

When your typing reaches the right-hand margin, indicated by the symbol on the top border, just keep typing and PipeDream will automatically carry words over to the start of the next line.

<Typed>You are invited to a party to celebrate the creation of my first document with the Cambridge Z88 computer. Champagne will be provided.</Typed>

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/pipedream-invitation2.webp" alt="pipedream-invitation2.png" width={670} />

## Adding text

PipeDream starts in Insert mode, although you also have the option of working in another mode called Overtype mode. You can change modes with the **Insert/Overtype** command.

In Insert mode, text is always added at the position indicated by the cursor. You can add text simply by moving the cursor to the point where you want the text added, and then typing. The cursor is moved around on your document using the four arrow keys, <Key k="left" />, <Key k="right" />, <Key k="up" />, <Key k="down" /> at the bottom right-hand side of the keyboard.

Position the cursor after the word <strong>`champagne`</strong>, on the second line of the text you have just entered, press the space bar, and add the phrase:

<Typed>and smoked salmon</Typed>

The original text will move apart to make room for the new text, and words will wrap around to the next line at the right-hand margin to ensure that the altered text is correctly formatted.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/pipedream-invitation3.webp" alt="pipedream-invitation3.png" width={670} />

## Starting a new paragraph

After adding the new text press the <Key k="enter" /> key. <Key k="enter" /> moves the cursor to the beginning of the next line.

Press <Key k="enter" /> twice more to leave a blank line and move the cursor down to row 5 on the page. Type the following:

`Sunday 28th February at 12 o'clock.`

You can use the <Key k="enter" /> key to end paragraphs, insert blank lines, and separate items in a list.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/pipedream-invitation4.webp" alt="pipedream-invitation4.png" width={670} />

## Columns

The page you are working on is divided into six columns, each 12 characters wide, shown by the rows of dots and the letters A to F on the top border of the screen. These provide the equivalent to tab positions on a typewriter.

With the cursor still on row 5, press the <Key k="tab" /> key exactly five times. The cursor will move to the left-hand edge of each column in turn until it reaches the left-hand side of the column labelled 'F'. Notice that as the cursor moves into each blank slot, text overlapping the slot is hidden. Now type in the text:

`RSVP`

The text will be positioned near the right-hand side of the sheet, opposite the text already on row 5.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/pipedream-invitation5.webp" alt="pipedream-invitation5.png" width={670} />

You can move to the previous tab position by holding down the <Key k="shift" /> key and pressing <Key k="tab" />. Try doing this to move the cursor back into column A, at the left- hand side of the screen.

## Replacing text

You can remove characters from the middle of a line by using the Delete Character command which you can give either using the EDIT menu, or by holding down the <Key k="shift" /> key and pressing <Key k="del" />.

Now you decide to change the day of the party to Saturday 28th February. Move the cursor across to the <strong>`S`</strong> of <strong>`Sunday`</strong> with the cursor keys. Then, hold down the <Key k="shift" /> key and press <Key k="del" />.

The <strong>`S`</strong> will be deleted. Keep pressing <Key k="shift" /> <Key k="del" /> until the word <strong>`Sunday`</strong> is removed. Then, type in the word `Saturday` to replace it.

## Inserting a line

Now add a heading to the invitation. To do this, you first need to insert two blank lines at the top of the page.

Move the cursor up to the first line of your document, row 1, by pressing <Key k="up" />.

Now give the **Insert Row** command. You can do this either using the **EDIT** menu, or by typing <Key k="diamond" />N. A blank line will be added where the cursor was, and the remaining lines of the document will be moved down on the page.

Type <Key k="diamond" />N again to give two blank lines at the top of the page. Then type in the following text:

`A Once-in-a-lifetime Party`

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/pipedream-invitation6.webp" alt="pipedream-invitation6.png" width={670} />

To emphasise the heading of the invitation, move the cursor to the beginning of the heading with the <Key k="left" /> key. Then type <Key k="diamond" />PB. When you move the cursor onto another line, the effect of each highlight is shown on the screen, just as it will appear when printed out. (Italic text is displayed as tiny characters.)

## Altering the layout of text

You can alter the layout of one or more lines of text in your document with one of the **Align** commands on the **LAYOUT** menu. For example, to centre a line of text, you move the cursor onto that line and then select the **Centre Align** command from the **LAYOUT** menu. Alternatively, you can type <Key k="diamond" />LAC.

Move the cursor onto the line containing the heading of the party invitation, if it is not there already, and type <Key k="diamond" />LAC. You will see the heading become centred on the page map (which always shows you the format of the page as it will appear when printed). The actual line of text will be centred on the document when you move the cursor onto the next line.

## Saving your document

Once you have finished working on a document it is a good idea to save it as a file to the Cambridge Z88 RAM. You will then be able to find it again without difficulty at a later date, and there is the added advantage that documents saved to the filing system take up less space and are more secure than documents left within PipeDream.

You can save a copy of your document into the Cambridge Z88 filing system by selecting **Save** from the **FILES** menu:

1. Press the <Key k="menu" /> key until the **FILES** menu is displayed on the screen.

2. Press the <Key k="down" /> key to highlight the **Save** command.

3. Press <Key k="enter" /> to select the **Save** command.

will be displayed at the top of the display, to give you the chance to go back and save your changes. Press <strong><Typed>Y</Typed></strong> to clear the document.

## Selecting the filename using the Filer

There is an easier way of selecting a file to load into a Z88 Application.

Instead of typing in the filename, the filename may be selected directly using the name from the Filer. This method may be used with most built-in Z88 applications like the Diary, PrinterEd, Panel, as well as some 3rd party software where the programmers have implemented this function like Unzip and Wordmongers XModem.

This method can be quicker and avoids the message as shown below in the Pipedream example, when a file named "wrong filename" which has not been saved is used.

## Printing the document

If you have a printer connected to the Cambridge Z88, you can print out a copy of the invitation with the following procedure:

1. Select the **PRINT** menu by pressing the <Key k="menu" /> key until **PRINT** is highlighted on the Menu List.

2. The **Print** operation may already be highlighted as it is the first item on the menu; otherwise use the cursor keys to select it.

3. Press <Key k="enter" /> to give the **Print** command.

4. Press <Key k="enter" /> again to ignore the **Print** options and print the invitation.

You can repeat the procedure to print as many copies as you want.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/using-pipedream/designing-a-party-invitation/047.webp" alt="Illustration for “Designing a party invitation”" width={600} />

To get bold and underlined text to print correctly you may need to use the Printer Editor; see **[Section Nine - The Printer Editor](/en/library/cambridge-z88/user-guide/the-printer-editor)**

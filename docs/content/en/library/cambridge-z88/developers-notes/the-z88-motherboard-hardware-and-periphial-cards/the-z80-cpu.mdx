<strong>*Z80 Clocks*</strong>

Two clocks are driving the Z80. MCK, the master clock and SCK, the standby clock. The MCK (3.2768 MHz) is generated by a 9.8304 Xtal to the Blink and divided by 3, given to the pin 6. The SCK pulses at 25.6 KHz and is active on COMA state.

<strong>*Interrupts*</strong>

There are three pins for dealing with interrupts : <br />BUSRQ (Bus Request) : used for DMA (not connected on the Z88) <br />NMI (Non Maskable Interrupt) : Jumps to $0066 (Battery failure, card insertion by a SNS low)<br />INT (Ordinary Interrupt) : used in mode 1 (IM1)

For dealing with the maskable interruptions (INT), the Z80 can be switch in three modes. <br />The interrupt mode 0 (IM0) : for 8080 compatibility <br />The interrupt mode 1 (IM1) : for non-zilog environment (our case) <br />The interrupt mode 2 (IM2) : for zilog environment

On reset, OZ puts the Z80 in IM1. <br />If interrupts are enabled every INT signal jumps to $0038. <br />These vector routines deals with the serial port, the keyboard, the bleep, the small timer, RTC and alarms.

# Recording the screen

Klive records the emulator's screen and sound as a video file, for gameplay clips, tutorials or a
demonstration of your program. It can also record the IDE and the emulator side by side.

<Callout type="info" emoji="💡">
FFmpeg is bundled with Klive, so there is nothing to install. On a platform for which no FFmpeg build
is bundled (such as Windows on ARM), the record button and the video items are hidden.
</Callout>

## Record in three steps

<Steps>

### Choose the format

**Machine › Record › Recording Settings...** opens the **Recording** page of the
[Settings](/en/guides/customizing/settings/). Choose the format, quality and frame rate there: they
are locked while a recording runs.

### Arm the recorder

Click **Start video recording** on the emulator toolbar, or choose
**Machine › Record › Start Video Recording**. If the machine is not running, the recorder is now
*armed*: the button's tooltip says **Ready – waiting for machine to run**. {/* prose-ok: the tooltip's own text */}

### Run the machine

Start or continue the machine. Recording begins with its first frame, and the tooltip says
**Recording... (click to stop)**.

</Steps>

To finish, click the button again (or choose **Machine › Record › Stop Video Recording**). The file
is written at once.

## The record button's states

| State | Tooltip | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Idle | **Start video recording** | A click arms the recorder, or starts recording if the machine runs. |
| Armed | **Ready – waiting for machine to run** | Recording starts when the machine runs. A click cancels. {/* prose-ok: the tooltip's own text */} |
| Recording | **Recording... (click to stop)** | Frames and sound are captured. A click stops and saves. |
| Paused | **Recording paused (click to stop)** | The machine is paused (for example at a breakpoint), or you paused the recording. |

The button's arrow (**Recordings**) starts the IDE + Emulator recording and opens the recording
settings.

## Pausing

- When the **machine pauses** (a breakpoint, a step, **Pause**), the recording pauses with it and
  continues when the machine runs again.
- **Machine › Record › Pause Video Recording** and **Continue Video Recording** pause the capture
  while the machine keeps running, to leave out a part you do not want.

The file stays open during a pause, so the video continues without a break.

## Recording options

These are on the **Recording** page of the Settings and can be changed only while no recording runs.

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Format** | **MP4** (H.264 + AAC, the default): plays everywhere. **WebM** (VP9 + Opus): for the web. **MKV** (H.265 + AAC): the smallest files, for modern players. |
| **Quality** | **Best compression** (the default, FFmpeg CRF 18): near-lossless, moderate files. **High** (CRF 10). **Highest (lossless)** (CRF 0): very large files, for editing later. |
| **Frame rate** | **The machine's own** (the default: 50 fps on a PAL machine, 60 on NTSC), or **Half**, which keeps sound and picture in step at half the size. |

<Callout type="warning" emoji="⚠️">
Lossless recordings are enormous: a minute of a ZX Spectrum can take several hundred megabytes.
</Callout>

The machine's sound (beeper, AY chips and the rest) goes into the same file. A machine that makes no
sound gives a silent video.

## Recording the IDE and the emulator together

To show your code and the running machine in one video, record both windows side by side: choose
**Machine › Record › Start IDE + Emulator recording**, or press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F7</kbd>
(the Control key on macOS too). Both window titles show **● REC**. The same command or shortcut stops
it. The IDE window must be visible to start.

This recording runs on the clock, not on the machine: it starts at once and keeps recording while the
machine is stopped or paused, carrying silence while the machine makes no sound.

The two windows are placed next to each other in the video, wherever they are on your screen. The
**IDE + Emulator recording** group of the Recording settings has:

| Option | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IDE position** | Left | Where the IDE goes: **Left**, **Right**, **Top** or **Bottom** of the emulator. The smaller window is centred along the shared side, on your theme's background. |
| **Include the pointer** | On | Draws the mouse pointer while it is over either window. |
| **Show mouse clicks** | On | Draws a ring around the pointer while a button is held, in the accent colour for the left button and the secondary accent for the right. Needs **Include the pointer**. |
| **Full resolution (HiDPI)** | Off | Records at the display's full pixel density: larger files and more work for the encoder. |

The format and quality apply here too; the frame rate is 30 fps (15 with **Half**). A window resized
during the recording is scaled to the space it had at the start.

Only one recording runs at a time: while one runs, the other's start item is unavailable.

To change the shortcut, set `shortcuts.recordIdeEmu` with the [`set`](/en/reference/commands#set)
command, for example `set -u shortcuts.recordIdeEmu "CmdOrCtrl+Shift+R"`. See
[Keyboard shortcuts](/en/guides/customizing/shortcuts/).

## Where the files go

Each recording is written to `KliveExports/video` in your home folder, named after the time it
started:

```text
~/KliveExports/video/recording_YYYYMMDD_HHMMSS.mp4
```

## Tips

- **Arm before you start.** The recording then has the machine's very first frame.
- **Keep long recordings small** with **Half** and **Best compression**.
- **Editing later?** Record lossless, in MKV for the smallest lossless file.

# Line editing

In most situations where you are entering a line of text in the Cambridge Z88, such as in response to a prompt, the following commands are available to make corrections and alterations to what you are typing:

<tbody>
<Table.Tr>

<Table.Td>moves the cursor left and right</Table.Td>
</Table.Tr>
<Table.Tr>
<Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: "nowrap" }}><Key k="diamond" /><Key k="left" />,<Key k="diamond" /><Key k="right" /></Table.Td>
<Table.Td>moves the cursor to the beginning or end of a line</Table.Td>
</Table.Tr>
<Table.Tr>
<Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: "nowrap" }}><Key k="shift" /><Key k="left" />, <Key k="shift" /> <Key k="right" /></Table.Td>
<Table.Td>moves the cursor to the previous or next word of a line</Table.Td>
</Table.Tr>
<Table.Tr>
<Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: "nowrap" }}><Key k="del" /></Table.Td>
<Table.Td>deletes the previous character</Table.Td>
</Table.Tr>
<Table.Tr>
<Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: "nowrap" }}><Key k="shift" /> <Key k="del" /></Table.Td>
<Table.Td>deletes the character at the cursor position</Table.Td>
</Table.Tr>
<Table.Tr>
<Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: "nowrap" }}><Key k="diamond" />D</Table.Td>

</Table.Tr>
<Table.Tr>
<Table.Td style={{ whiteSpace: "nowrap" }}><Key k="diamond" />T</Table.Td>

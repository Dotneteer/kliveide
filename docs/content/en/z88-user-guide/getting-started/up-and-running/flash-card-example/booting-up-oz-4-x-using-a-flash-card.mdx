**1.** Open the Card Flap and push the card into Slot 1.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card-hard-reset.webp" alt="z88 512 512 card hard reset" width={640} />

**2.** Leaving the Card Flap open (or open it again), press the reset switch a couple of times with an opened paperclip.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card-shut-card-flap.webp" alt="z88 512 512 card shut card flap" width={640} />

**3.** Shut the Card Flap

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/external/zp-memory-chips-testing-and-results/hard-reset.webp" alt="hard reset" width={640} />

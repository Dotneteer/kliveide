HT-1080Z ROMs (ht1080z-s1*.rom, ht1080z-s2*.rom, ht1080z-s64*.rom)

These are the ROMs of the HT-1080Z, the Hungarian school computer made by
Hiradastechnika Szovetkezet from 1983: the Level II BASIC (Microsoft), the
extension EPROM (EACA and Hiradastechnika Szovetkezet) and the character
generator of each model. The rights belong to Microsoft, EACA and
Hiradastechnika Szovetkezet, or to their successors.

No explicit permission to redistribute them is known. They are included,
unmodified, because the HT preservation group publishes them openly, so
that the emulated machine works without the user having to find them. They
will be removed on request from a rights holder.

Source: the HT preservation site, https://ht.homeserver.hu/html/hardverrom.html
(files under https://ht.homeserver.hu/bin/rom/). The dumps are credited to
Majzik Istvan. Taken on 2026-10-22. Every image matches the CRC32 and SHA1
of the corresponding MAME set (ht1080z, ht1080z2, ht108064).

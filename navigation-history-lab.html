Model inspector (lab only)

    Try this
    1. In main.asm click PrintString (Go to Definition), then PutChar.
    2. Press Ctrl+- or Alt+← twice, then Forward (Ctrl+Shift+- / Alt+→). Mouse buttons 4/5 work too.
    3. Step the debugger (F10 button) a few times — pauses are not recorded. Click PutChar while paused, then Back returns to the paused line, and Back again to where you were before debugging.
    4. Open Memory and type 5B00 in Go to. Scrolling with the wheel is not recorded.
    5. In game.nex open bank 12, click DrawTiles then PlaySfx (another bank).
    6. Open sprites.spr — it has no navigation adapter, so Back goes to where you left.

- The largest T4 gain is debugger/instruction-bounded execution: avoiding full

  records 51,200 trace events instead of 102,400 because audio-only border
  duplicates are skipped.
- Separate static event buffers were considered but not introduced. The current
  event buffer already has static per-trace slices, and preserving the ABI is
  preferable until trace-buffer locality is proven to dominate.

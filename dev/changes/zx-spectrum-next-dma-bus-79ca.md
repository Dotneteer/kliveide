**ZX Spectrum Next DMA Bus Timeline.** A new **DMA Bus Timeline** document (Debug › DMA Bus Timeline, the DMA panel's **Open the bus timeline** link, or `show-dma-timeline`) records when the DMA holds the bus while it is open: an overview of the DMA's share of each frame, with a mark on frames whose pulse-mode interrupt was lost while the DMA held the bus; the selected frame to scale in T-states, the DMA's holds against the CPU's time, with zoom; and its figures, including the measured byte period of a paced transfer.

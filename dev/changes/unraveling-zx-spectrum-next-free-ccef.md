**Unraveling the ZX Spectrum Next, free on kliveide.net:** István Novák's 341-page book on the Next's hardware, as a programmer sees it, can be downloaded as a PDF together with its examples, a Klive IDE project of 27 demos built into one `.nex` program. Every landing page promotes it in its first screen, and the book's own page, kliveide.net/unraveling-book, lists its parts, chapters and demos and shows how to run the examples in Klive or on a real Next.

**Floating document windows:** right-click a document's tab and choose **Pop Out to Window** to move
it into a window of its own, which you can move and resize freely (onto a second screen, next to the
emulator). Its title bar has **Dock**, which puts the document back into the editor area it came
from, and **Close**. Breakpoints, the execution point, the editor's keys and the menu's shortcuts
work there as in the IDE; Go Back and Go Forward share one history with the IDE, and opening the
document again brings its window to the front. Up to 4 documents can float, and the project reopens
them in their windows.

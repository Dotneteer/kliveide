**Enterprise 128 ROM annotations, EXOS 2.1 segment 01h:** the second half of EXOS 2.1 is annotated too, with a label on every call and jump target and a synopsis for each of its 923 labels: IS-BASIC 2.1's added functions and statements (maths, strings, SET, LIST, RENUMBER, AUTO, PRINT USING, the packed error messages), the clock, the PRINTER, TAPE, SERIAL and NET devices, and the Enterprise Word Processor. The Disassembly view now lists IS-BASIC's call lists (the routine bytes after RST 10h) as data on that segment, each byte named, instead of reading them as instructions.

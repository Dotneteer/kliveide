**Z88 card images choose their card.** In the Insert Z88 Card dialog, select the card image first and Klive selects a card type that fits its size: a 32K, 128K or 256K UV EPROM up to that size, then a 512K or 1M AMD Flash. You can still pick another type. An image smaller than its card, such as a 16K application, now goes at the top of a larger card instead of being refused.

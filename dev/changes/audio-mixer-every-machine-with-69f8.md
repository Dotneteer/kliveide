**Audio mixer:** every machine with sound now lists its sound sources (the beeper, each AY channel,
the Next's TurboSound channels, DACs and Pi I2S input, the Z88's speaker, the TVC's sound generator
and the Enterprise's DAVE channels) with a live level meter and spectrum, and lets you mute, solo or
change the level of each one. Open it with **View › Machine Views › Audio Mixer** (or
`show-audio-mixer`) for an analyser with spectrum, scope and spectrogram, or from the arrow beside
the emulator's sound button for quick mutes. The settings are kept for each machine type and never
change the emulated machine: saved states and stepping back are unaffected.

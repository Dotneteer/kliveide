## Booting up OZ 4.x using a Flash Card

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card.webp" alt="New Z88 512K/512K Flash/RAM card" width={640} />

Here is a picture of the 512K/512K Flash/RAM card out of the packaging.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card-and-instructions.webp" alt="z88 512 512 card and instructions" width={640} />

There are even instructions with it. Does anybody read these?

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card-into-z88.webp" alt="z88 512 512 card into z88" width={640} />

**1.** Open the Card Flap and push the card into Slot 1.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card-hard-reset.webp" alt="z88 512 512 card hard reset" width={640} />

**2.** Leaving the Card Flap open (or open it again), press the reset switch a couple of times with an opened paperclip.

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/getting-started/up-and-running/flash-card-example/z88-512-512-card-shut-card-flap.webp" alt="z88 512 512 card shut card flap" width={640} />

**3.** Shut the Card Flap

<Shot src="z88-user-guide/external/zp-memory-chips-testing-and-results/hard-reset.webp" alt="hard reset" width={640} />

You should see the HARD RESET ... come up on the screen, if not

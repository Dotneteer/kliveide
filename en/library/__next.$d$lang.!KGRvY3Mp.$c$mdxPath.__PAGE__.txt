# Library

The Library holds the manuals of the machines Klive emulates: their own documentation, written by
their makers and communities, not by the Klive project. Read them to learn a machine itself, its
operating system and its applications, or to write programs for it. How to use the machine in Klive
is in the [Guides](/en/guides/), under [Other Machines](/en/guides/machines/zx81-zx80/).

## Cambridge Z88

The Z88's manuals are imported from the [Cambridge Z88 wiki](https://cambridgez88.jira.com/wiki/).
Running the Z88 in Klive is covered in [The Cambridge Z88](/en/guides/machines/cambridge-z88/).

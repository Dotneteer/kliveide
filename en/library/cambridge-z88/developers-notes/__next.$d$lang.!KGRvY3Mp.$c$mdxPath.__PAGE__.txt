# Z88 Developers' Notes

<Callout type="info">

These are the **Z88 Developers' Notes**, imported from the [Cambridge Z88 wiki](https://cambridgez88.jira.com/wiki/spaces/DN/overview): how to write applications for the Z88 and its OZ operating system, its system calls, and its hardware. The pages follow the original notes' structure.

</Callout>

Welcome to the Z88 Developers' Notes.

<Shot src="z88-developers-notes/welcome/developers-notes.webp" alt="developers notes" width={880} />

The original text were published by Cambridge Computer in 1988, which could be bought separately or as part of a commercial Z88 application development kit. Matthew Soar has since then published them here: [http://www.cix.co.uk/\~ponda/Z88dev093.htm](http://www.cix.co.uk/~ponda/Z88dev093.htm).

<Callout type="default">

**Cambridge Computer Team, 1988-1989**

Editor:<br />**[Matthew Soar](http://www.cix.co.uk/~ponda/)**

Authors:<br />**[John Harrison<br />](http://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/users/jrh/)****[Matthew Elton](http://www.stir.ac.uk/philosophy/staff/elton/home.html)**

Consultants:<br />**Trinity Concepts Ltd,** **Jim Westwood &** **Graham French**

</Callout>

The Z88 Project Development team picked up the text from V2 and continued to improve it, according to new developments in OZ.

**Z88 Development Project Team since 1995**

Authors:<br />**[Gunther Strube](mailto:hello@bits4fun.net)**, **Thierry Peycru** & **[Dennis Gröning](http://www.algonet.se/~dennisgr/)**

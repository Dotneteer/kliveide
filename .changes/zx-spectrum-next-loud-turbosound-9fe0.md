On the ZX Spectrum Next, loud TurboSound music was cut off: Klive's mixer ran out of range at about two AY chips at full volume, while the Next plays all three cleanly. The mix now has the same room above silence as the Next's audio jack, so everything the jack plays cleanly plays cleanly in Klive; the Next's sound is somewhat quieter than before as a result.

**The 128K's editor ROM fully annotated:** the ZX Spectrum 128K's editor ROM (ROM 0) now has annotations of its own, at the same completeness as the 48K ROM's: every routine is labelled and described and its lines are commented, from start-up and the main menu to the full-screen editor and its menus, Renumber, the Tape Tester, the RAM disk, PLAY and MIDI, the RS232 and printer commands, and the tape commands. Export as source reassembles it byte for byte.

**Completeness level hover card in the ROM annotation editor:** rest the pointer on the readiness chip, the "records level" text, the Completeness column head or a page's level meter, and a card explains the three levels, marks which ones are reached and which comes next, and counts what is still missing: the call and jump targets without a label and the labels without a synopsis. The card opens after a short pause, stays open while you move onto it, and closes on Escape; it also opens when the keyboard focuses one of those places.

**ZX Spectrum Next sound outputs.** The Next sends its sound to an audio jack, an internal speaker and HDMI, each built differently; Klive now plays the one you choose under Machine › Sound › Output (or Settings › Emulator): the audio jack by default, where a very loud mix wraps around as on the real jack, the internal speaker with its mono mix, its on/off switch and its beeper-only mode, or HDMI with the whole sum, sampled as HDMI does and silenced by its own switch.

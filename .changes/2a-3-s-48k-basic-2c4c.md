**The +2A/+3's 48K BASIC ROMs fully annotated:** ROM 3 of the ZX Spectrum +2A and +3, in the v4.0, v4.1 and Spanish versions (the +3E's ROM 3 is v4.0's), now has annotations of its own at the same completeness as the 48K ROM's. Each takes the 48K and 128K ROMs' labels and comments wherever its bytes are the same, and describes what Amstrad changed: the disk motor's time-out in the frame interrupt, the NMI, the routines that switch to another ROM, the hooks into the editor ROM (which v4.1 turns off), and the messages, including the Spanish texts and characters. Export as source reassembles each one byte for byte.

**The 128K's ROM 1 fully annotated:** the ZX Spectrum 128K's ROM 1 now has annotations of its own, at the same completeness as the 48K ROM's. It takes the 48K ROM's labels and comments wherever its bytes are the same, and describes what the 128K changed: the six routines it patched, and the new code where the 48K ROM is empty, such as the keypad routines, the hooks that pass control to the 128K's other ROM, and the SPECTRUM and PLAY keywords. ROM annotations that build on another ROM's now work everywhere: the Disassembly view, the ROM annotation editor's readiness check and completeness level, and Export as source, which reassembles ROM 1 byte for byte.

**Tapes on the ZX Spectrum Next.** Insert a .tap or .tzx with Machine › Tape › Insert, and it loads whenever a program uses the 48K ROM's tape routines, as LOAD "" in the Next's 48K BASIC does: with fast load at any CPU speed, or in real time at 3.5 MHz, through the Next's own EAR input (it drops pulses shorter than about 1.14 µs and lets a level held at 1 fall to 0 after about 1.15 ms), and heard as on the real machine. A SAVE is captured and offered as a .tzx.

**ZX Spectrum accuracy, published:** a new documentation page, "ZX Spectrum: how Klive's accuracy is tested", lists the community's timing test programs Klive's 48K, 128K and +2A/+3 pass - ZX Spectrum Timing Tests 48K and 128K, HALT2INT, Float48K, FloatSpy, Border Time, Screen Time, Timing Test, the ULA 48/128/128E +3 picture tests and IR Contention - with what each one checks and Klive's own screen running it. HALT2INT's results stand beside its author's photos of a real early and a real late 48K, the picture tests are checked against real machines' photos, and the page says what is not proven yet. The landing page links to it under "Tested against real hardware".

**Enterprise 64 ROM annotations, EXOS 2.0:** both segments of the Enterprise 64's EXOS 2.0 are annotated, with a label on every call and jump target and a synopsis for each of their 2,041 labels: the kernel, VIDEO, SOUND, KEYBOARD, EDITOR and PRINTER in segment 00h, and IS-BASIC 2.0's routines, the clock, TAPE, SERIAL, NET and the word processor in segment 01h. They were carried over from EXOS 2.1's annotations where the code is the same and written for 2.0 where it differs. On the 64, the IS-BASIC call lists in segment 01h are listed as data too.

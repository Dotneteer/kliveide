**Enterprise IS-BASIC 2.1 ROM annotations:** the IS-BASIC 2.1 cartridge is annotated too, with a label on every call and jump target and a synopsis for each of its 1,021 labels: the start-up and the gate that lets IS-BASIC call EXOS segment 1, the tokeniser and the lister, the statements and their keyword table, the expression evaluator, the decimal arithmetic, arrays, user-defined functions, the error handling and the graphics and sound statements. Its call lists after RST 10h are listed as data, each routine byte named.

**Late ULA timing:** the ZX Spectrum 16K, 48K and 128K can run with the "late" (alternate) ULA timing of many real machines, everything the ULA does one T-state later against the interrupt. Switch it on with **Machine › ZX Spectrum 48K / 128K › Late ULA Timing** or in Settings › Emulator; an `.szx` snapshot sets the timing its machine ran, and saving one records it.
